Government Schemes Hub 🏛

Direct links and guidelines for active agricultural support schemes.

PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi

An initiative by the Government of India that provides up to ₹6,000 per year in three equal installments to small and marginal farmers.

Eligibility: Land-holding farmer families with cultivable land holdings in their names.
Benefits: ₹6,000 direct benefit transfer bank deposits annually.
Required Documents: Aadhaar Card, Land ownership papers, Bank account details.
Apply on Official Portal
PMFBY

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

An actuarial premium based crop insurance scheme offering comprehensive risk coverage against crop failure due to non-preventable natural risks.

Eligibility: All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas, including tenant farmers.
Benefits: Insurance claims coverage for post-harvest loss, localized calamities, and dry-spells.
Required Documents: Land records, sowing certificate, identity proof.
Apply on Official Portal
